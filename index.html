01 — The Shop

A classic barbershop feel — and the regulars to prove it.

The walls are covered in music posters. The chair has history. Reviews say it over and over — “classic barbershop feel” is the most-used phrase in all 267 of them.

Lovejoy’s sits in a converted house on Savannah Highway — reviewers will tell you it’s a hidden gem on the back side of the building, and worth finding the first time. Appointments book out around the big moments: clients have come in the week of their wedding for the cut and come back two days before for the touch-up.

Black-owned and proudly everyone’s shop — kids welcome, everyone welcome.
